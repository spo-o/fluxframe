How the bits became music

    Each "field → value" is the bit slice's integer value, used to pick a key, scale, chord progression, drum pattern, rhythm template, and chord-tone palette from curated musical options. The bits are physical; every output is on-beat and harmonic by construction.

    Why this matters

    Most generative music is fake-random.

    Math.random() is deterministic. Same seed, same song. Generative music libraries that "feel" surprising are running PRNGs you could replay byte-for-byte from any clock value.

    Beacon bits can't be replayed.

    Each NIST pulse mixes entropy from independent hardware sources (including quantum RNGs) and is signed and chained to the previous pulse. A pulse cannot be revised, predicted, or forged after the fact.

    Random ≠ musical. Selection from the random fixes that.

    Raw entropy mapped to per-step on/off doesn't groove. Quantum Score uses bits to select between musical building blocks — chord progressions, drum patterns, rhythm templates, chord-tone palettes — so every output is harmonic and on-beat. The entropy is still physical; the form is still musical.
